## 4. Accessibility and inclusive-use findings

WCAG-01 is the baseline reference. The report calls for equivalent non-voice pathways for every essential task and records these design requirements:

|---|---|

| Speech disability | Text-based requests, selectable AAC phrases and optional written follow-up; no mandatory phone call. |
| Motor disability | Large touch targets, keyboard support, no time-limited actions, forgiving error recovery. |
| Cognitive disability | Plain Bangla, one action per screen, progress indicators, repeated key information. |

### Bangladesh privacy law — legal review required

The Master Report says the Personal Data Protection Ordinance, 2025 (Ordinance No. 61 of 2025, dated 6 November 2025) **was repealed** by the Personal Data Protection Act, 2026 (Act No. 63 of 2026). The supplied legislative database URL, https://bdlaws.minlaw.gov.bd/act-1574.html, is recorded for the historical Ordinance and is not treated here as a verified link to the current Act. The current Act's primary text and its duties, section numbers, enforcement mechanisms and applicability remain to be obtained and mapped.

The report describes consent, sensitive data, data-subject rights, security/breach, retention, audit and cross-border-transfer matters as subjects that require current-Act mapping. Its product recommendations are to use clear, informed and revocable consent; treat pregnancy, disability, mental-health and contact data as sensitive by default; provide understandable rights and a documented process; use role-based access, encryption in transit, audit logs and incident response; define purpose-bound retention/deletion; and obtain legal review before cross-border transfers. These are product recommendations, not confirmed statutory duties. **Bangladesh-qualified legal review is mandatory before legal publication.**
