# PROHOR Barta · Day 1 source register

Owner: Iftee. Sources checked 16 November 2026. Status: draft for review. This register contains four references and no approved retrieval passages.

## Initial scope

- **ANC terminology.** Explain what ANC stands for and describe its general purpose in plain Bangla or English.
- **Routine visit preparation.** Help users prepare questions and understand what a care provider may ask. Keep facility-specific procedures and appointment schedules outside this first scope.

## What to record for each passage before ingestion

Use one record per bounded passage. Record its stable passage ID, source ID, exact section or page, edition or snapshot date, URL, original and reviewed display languages, jurisdiction, narrow claims it can support, reviewer, review date, usage terms and the decision. A Bangla paraphrase should be checked against the original passage. Usage terms are pending for all four inventory entries. Record permission or the applicable basis for storing each passage before indexing it.

- **Candidate** means a passage is awaiting scope, language, clinical applicability, and reuse review.
- **Reference only** means the document supplies research context and stays outside retrieval.
- **Approved** means a named reviewer has accepted the bounded passage for its stated use and its use terms are recorded.

Save evidence at passage level so the API can attach citations from retrieval results. If a reviewed passage cannot support a claim, Barta should abstain. Keep the retrieval index empty until at least one passage qualifies.
