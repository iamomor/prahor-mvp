## C. Exact source passages and page references

### Pregnancy

The original WHO PCPNC PDF’s C15 section is on **PDF p. 44** (text extraction marker P43; printed folio C15). It gives immediate care-seeking wording for the pregnancy signs listed in MAT-001–006. In the same section, swelling and several other signs are assigned “as soon as possible” care instead. The source is an international guide for skilled attendants, and the text itself says it is generic and should be adapted to local needs and resources. [WHO PCPNC catalog](https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789241549356), [original WHO PCPNC PDF](https://iris.who.int/bitstream/handle/10665/249580/9789241549356-eng.pdf)

For MAT-007, the same PDF’s C7 provider algorithm on p. 44 distinguishes no fetal movement with no fetal heartbeat from no movement when a heartbeat is present. It does **not** establish a public instruction for reduced fetal movement. C15’s “waters have broken” advice supports a facility/skilled-attendant contact, but does not define timing for every type of fluid leakage. C8 on p. 44 addresses burning urination in a provider algorithm; action differs with accompanying fever/flank findings.

For MAT-009, B2 on PDF p. 22 lists severe vomiting in an emergency quick check. This is provider triage, not a public warning-sign text or a validated duration threshold.

### Labour and childbirth

The DGHS *Operational Manual for Maternal and Newborn Health Service Accreditation Program*, Edition 2022, lists admission danger signs in its intrapartum section (printed p. 32) and includes a Bangla danger-sign panel in Annexure 3 (printed p. 111). The panel gives a general “take to a health centre quickly” instruction. These are useful Bangladesh source locators, but the manual’s clinical text cites the Maternal Health SOP and emergency-care flowcharts; it is not itself proof that every phrase is a current, reviewed public content standard. [DGHS manual PDF](https://objectstorage.ap-dcc-gazipur-1.oraclecloud15.com/n/axvjbnqprylg/b/V2Ministry/o/office-dghs/2024/12/e05fb1bf1ad947d69b6ebaf99a42961f.pdf)

WHO PCPNC’s D7 birth-companion section is on PDF p. 63 and calls for help when there is vaginal bleeding, sudden worsening pain, loss of consciousness or fits. D4–D5 on the same page and D8 on PDF p. 71 are provider protocols, not patient-facing definitions of “labour that is not progressing.”

### Postpartum

WHO PCPNC D28 is on PDF p. 64. It explicitly separates its immediate signs from signs for which the woman should go to a health centre as soon as possible. The source’s postpartum bleeding threshold is tied to the stated **20–30-minute** period and to bleeding that increases rather than decreases. The separate D22 immediate-postpartum provider chart, PDF p. 94, uses a different clinical assessment context. These thresholds must not be combined or detached from their contexts.

The M4 counseling sheet on PDF p. 165 supports postpartum severe headache with blurred vision and broadly mentions wound infection. It does not support adding the proposed wound symptom list as if each sign had been independently verified.

### Perinatal mental health

The WHO 2022 postnatal guideline supports routine postnatal care and maternal wellbeing; the 2022 perinatal mental-health integration guide concerns service integration, provider response and local/cultural adaptation. Neither establishes the exact wording of all four proposed mental-health titles or a Bangladesh mother-facing crisis route. [WHO postnatal guideline](https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789240045989), [WHO perinatal mental-health guide](https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789240057142)

The DGHS *Guideline for Case Identification, Management & Reporting for Forcibly Displaced Myanmar Nationals*, first edition June 2018, has a p. 153 section on psychological trauma. It directs providers to refer suicidal or self-harming clients to specialized services and says suicide threats require immediate referral. This is Bangladesh-specific provider guidance for a particular population; it does not name a referral destination or establish a public referral pathway for all mothers. [DGHS guideline PDF](https://csc.dghs.gov.bd/assets/downloads/case_identification.pdf)
